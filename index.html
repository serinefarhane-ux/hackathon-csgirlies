Our Mission

Our mission is to help you embody an active role in your lifestyle. Our goal is to help you make intentional choices that lead to a healthy and fulfilling life, whatever that may look like for you. Our website is designed to acknowledge the various emotions that can significantly impact your well-being. We are excited to help create more of a balance between physical and mental health care by encouraging users to practice mindfulness.

Why is this important?

Avoiding conversations and suppressing declining mental health can undermine cognitive development and physical performance.

Why Mindfulness

Those who practice mindfulness often have more control over how their focus is distributed. In a research study, scientists looked to analyze brain activation during mindful meditation in people used to practicing mindfulness and those who were completely new to it. In the experienced meditators, the Default Mode Network was less active than in the inexperienced meditators who reported that their attention wandered during the meditation. The researchers ultimately concluded that seasoned meditators tend to have better focus than inexperienced meditators. Our goal is to encourage our users to control their focus by practicing mindfulness regularly.

Our Vision

We hope that people use our product as a substitute for turning to social media for stress relief. Contrary to what many believe, social media can actually exacerbate stress. We hope our product will help users who want a space where they don’t feel pressured to suppress their emotions.
